Biography

About Me

I am an undergraduate student and researcher in training at the University of Rajshahi (Session 2024–2025). My academic focus resides at the intersection of quantitative demography, climate vulnerability, and policy framework development.

My objective is to leverage mathematical computation and demographic estimation models to understand the structural transformations caused by Industry 4.0 and environmental displacement in developing nations.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

Population Studies & Demography

Analyzing population structure, fertility trends, mortality rates, and formal demographic estimation techniques.

Climate Displacement

Studying internal socio-economic migration and resource reallocation driven by environmental changes in coastal zones.

Public Health

Evaluating healthcare accessibility, epidemiological dynamics, and human resource distribution in regional health systems.

Industry 4.0 & HRD

Assessing workforce adaptation, technological skill gaps, and strategic human resource development planning.

Statistical Modelling

Current Research

Proposal Stage / Ongoing Research

Uncharted Demographics: Assessing the Impact of Industry 4.0 Skill-Gaps and Climate Displacement on Human Resource Development in Bangladesh

This study investigates how concurrent pressures—specifically automation skill deficits and environmental forced migration—reshape national human resource capacity. The research aims to formulate quantitative frameworks for targeted policy interventions.

Primary Methodology: Quantitative Demographic Estimation, Statistical Cross-Sectional Analysis
